# 1MSO.Church

Welcome to One Missoula Church!

## About

- Know Jesus. Build His Kingdom.

### Mission

- The mission of One Missoula Church is the same mission Jesus gave his followers: Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.

### Vision

- The vision of One Missoula Church is to create a community of authentic believers and to witness a city transformed by the life changing reality of the gospel, as people come to know Jesus as their lord and savior.
